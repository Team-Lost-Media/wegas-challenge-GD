Super John
Origin: The Super John games
Super John gradually accelerates towards you, with a speedometer above his head. If he's fast enough (indicated by the speedometer text becoming red and the Super John Orange Line appearing), he can hit you, knocking you away and dealing 20 damage. If he knocks you away, Golden Sigma will NOT save you! [br] [br] Once he's at the speed treshold, the Super John Orange Line will appear, showing the direction he's headed in.
Rorys is actually more beneficial than he seems: his explosion when punched is extremely useful, so learning how to best make use of it is essential to "mastering" him. [br] [br]Keep in mind that the direction of the boost his explosion gives you is based off of where you're pointing the camera. More specifically, it'll send you in the OPPOSITE direction of your camera.
* His ability (taken from WC:TI) is based off of the original Golden Sigma GIF from where he originates from, where he's overlayed on top of Friend shaking and changing size. [br] [br]* "Is his name Rorys or Rory?" The answer is both. [br] [br]* The explosion sound and animation he uses are the same as Deltarune's. [br] [br]* He wants to kill Friend. [br] [br]* He is the main antagonist of the Super Golden Sigma games.
In WC:TI, Rorys can be problematic because you often dont have an easy way of avoiding him. In WEGAKILL, this is not the case. You can easily go around him, either by jumping to a different path or by straight up jumping over him. [br] [br]As a result, I wanted Rorys to be more interesting. When implementing him, I had (once again) taken inspiration from ULTRAKILL. ULTRAKILL has a weapon where, if you slam into an enemy at high speed, it'll deal massive damage and send you flying backwards. I LOVE this weapon. So, I decided to do something similar for Rorys, and this was the end result. [br] [br]Small bonus: since there's no concept of acceleration and deceleration while moving, I had to resort to preventing you from moving for a short period when the Rorys Explosion starts.
