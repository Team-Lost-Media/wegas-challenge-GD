Ultra Irios
Origin: The Super John games
Ultra Irios acts like Wega: he constantly moves towards your position and phases through obstacles. However, he moves at a slower speed. [br] [br]When touched, he instantly kills you. However, he also shoots fireballs at you that deal 40 damage when connecting.
Despite his significantly lower speed than Wega's, his ability to instakill you by touching you should never be underestimated. His fireballs aren't very difficult to avoid if you pay attention to them, however, if you're too close to Ultra Irios, they tend to connect far more frequently. Keeping your distance is often enough for the fireballs to miss you completely without you having to dodge.
* His ability (taken from WC:TI) is based off of the original Golden Sigma GIF from where he originates from, where he's overlayed on top of Friend shaking and changing size. [br] [br]* "Is his name Rorys or Rory?" The answer is both. [br] [br]* The explosion sound and animation he uses are the same as Deltarune's. [br] [br]* He wants to kill Friend. [br] [br]* He is the main antagonist of the Super Golden Sigma games.
