Super John
Origin: The Super John games
Super John gradually accelerates towards you, with a speedometer above his head. If he reaches the speed threshold of 20 units per second (indicated by the Super John Orange Line and by his speedometer becoming red) he can hit you, knocking you away and dealing 10 damage. [br] [br]Once he's at the speed treshold, the Super John Orange Line will appear, showing the direction he's headed in. [br] [br]Super John can be deflected by punching him with Left Click.
Super John can be very dangerous, as his knockback can make you fly off the map and send you into the death zone. On top of that, since he deals 10 damage on hit, he often removes all of your overheal before launching you, meaning that you can't tank the damage of the death zone. [br][br]Because of this, it's important to keep him in mind at all times so you can effectively deflect him whenever he approaches. Sometimes he may also miss, but it's not a good idea to rely on that as your sole source of survival against Super John.
 
I'm going to be frank with you: I think WC:TI's lap 2 is very flawed. Ultra Irios is an interesting sidegrade to Wega, but Golden Rorys is annoying more than anything and Fried Maltigi is just a souped up Maltigi. My main issue with it is Golden Rorys: a powered up version of Rorys that makes him chase after you like Wega after spawning, often closing in on you with no way to avoid besides tanking the distraction that is often guaranteed to kill you. He isn't fun to deal with, isn't enjoyable to juke, and is generally just a very uninspired enemy. Rorys, but stronger. [br][br]Later, during development of THE IDOL, I realized: this isn't a WC:TI clone. And I don't want it to be. WC:TI is a great game, but I don't want WEGAKILL to just be seen as a WC:TI fangame: I want it to be a WEGA game, not a WC:TI one. So, I decided to not just make THE IDOL "wcti but with wegakill movement", but to make it my own take on WC:TI, using WEGAKILL. This is what led to the Rorys Explosion, which is now one of my favourite parts of the game. And, later on, while developing Lap 2, I followed the same philosophy: not to make a WC:TI clone, but to make a mode inspired by my favourite parts of it. So, when I had the option to replace my least favourite parts of Lap 2 with my own ideas, I took it. This is what led to Super John. [br][br]Despite deciding to make new enemies for Lap 2, I struggled figuring out a suitable mechanic for one. (In fact, I struggled trying to think of a 3rd Lap 2 enemy for a while before settling on the current selection. Rorys got added because I just find him really fun, though I'm not the biggest fan of Fried Maltigi...) In the end, I decided to make Super John home into the player and shove them aside if he hits, an ability based off of his ramming move in Super John 2, and an ability I'm personally very proud of.
