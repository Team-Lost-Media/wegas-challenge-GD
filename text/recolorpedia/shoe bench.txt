I'm a big fan of music based events in games. So, when I got the opportunity to make such a thing for WEGAKILL, I took it instantly. [br][br]The way the music based events work is actually pretty simple: a variable is increased by 1 every time a timer that runs for exactly 1.311 seconds ends. This timer is actually special, as it ALWAYS runs, even during lagspikes or during hitstop. 1.311 seconds is the length of one bar of WEGAFADENCE, the music made by GAMR for WC:TI Lap 2 that is also used in this game's Lap 2. Originally, I used a plug-in made by someone else for this timer. However, it ended up being strangely janky, so, when I realized I had a way to make timers sync with the music by disassociating them from the game's FPS, I just switched to using a regular timer node.[br][br]I really enjoyed adding little touches to lap 2, such as the smooth transition and the music-timed bounce Shoe Bench does. Originally, the progress bar was meant to bounce too, but it looked bad.[br] [br]Good luck with +SHOE BENCH :)
