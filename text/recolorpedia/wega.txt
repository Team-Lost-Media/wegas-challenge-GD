Wega
Origin: idk
Wega constantly moves towards your position, phasing through obstacles. [br] [br]When touched, he instantly kills you.
Wega is constantly chasing you, and so he must be constantly kept track of. Luckily, since his behaviour is so basic, it's rather easy to keep track of him. [br] [br]On top of that, he also has varying tells on how close-by he is, such as the colored light he emits and the sound he constantly plays.
* His scream is a cut sound from Half-Life 2, called "hl2-stalker-scream.mp3". [br] [br]* Urio is his brother, and both run a site called MOVIEFREE.COM. [br] [br]* Go play WATLM
Wega is the main threat of the game, but, by himself, he's extremely easy to avoid (his chase in the tutorial should be proof of that). [br] [br]This is because Wega is most dangerous when there's other things pressuring you, such as the threat of falling, the need to rack up points or other enemies. [br] [br]The idea for "STYLE" came as a result of me adding jumping and dashing to Xetheon's port of Wega's Challenge to Godot. I found that juking around Wega with a dash was fun, and I wanted to incentivize doing so. I had also recently played ULTRAKILL, which was (and still is) a very big source of inspiration for this game's design.
