Maltigi
Origin: LOST MEDIA (unknown) (popularized on Twitter)
Maltigi will prepare to dash towards you, picking a position and creating a red line between him and the target position. After a short delay he will quickly dash towards that position. [br] [br]Touching Maltigi immediately kills you.
As long as you know where he will dash, which is very easy thanks to the Maltigi Red Line, Maltigi is very easy to handle. Occasionally he could perfectly predict your movement, but you have plenty of time to react and it rarely happens in the first place.
* Maltigi is really fucking evil. [br] [br]* He HATES Wega. He allies with him because both just wants to kill you. [br] [br]* Maltigi is frequently associated with LOST MEDIA [br] [br]* He is extremely homophobic. [br] [br]* He runs a scam call center.
Maltigi is my favourite enemy from WC:TI, so he was obviously going to be added, but implementing him was another story. [br] [br]First of all, I was CONVINCED he predicted the player's movement in WC:TI and decided to implement it. Doing so wasn't actually all that difficult, I just took the player's velocity and added it to the target position. However, he did not predict the player's movement. Instead, he overshoots his target. Implementing this was also rather easy (much easier than movement prediction, at least.) [br] [br]Instead, the most difficult part about coding Maltigi was the Maltigi Red Line. Maltigi makes a line when dashing to his target position. This is the Maltigi Red Line. I had no idea how to make this line. The only solution I "found" (got told by someone in the #quick-help channel in the Godot discord server) was to draw the line using an "ImmediateMesh", and this was how the game drew the line ever since it was added. That was until i discovered an add-on called "DebugDraw3D", which just so happens to let you easily draw lines in 3D space! it also has absolutely no documentation god dammit
