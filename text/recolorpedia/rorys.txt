Rorys
Origin: Golden Sigma "I will save you!" GIF
Rorys picks a random Wegadoll to spawn on, tinting it blue. After enough time, they'll appear on the Wegadoll. [br] [br]Touching Rorys will make you take 70 damage as they temporarily blind you. [br] [br]Punching Rorys with ATTACK (Left Click) will make them explode, stunning them and sending you backwards in the direction of your camera. This can be used for a speed boost, or to gain height.
Rorys is actually more beneficial than they seem: the explosion caused when when punching them is extremely useful, so learning how to best make use of it is essential to "mastering" Rorys. [br] [br]Keep in mind that the direction of the boost their explosion gives you is based off of where you're pointing the camera. More specifically, it'll send you in the OPPOSITE direction of your camera.
* Their ability (taken from WC:TI) is based off of the original Golden Sigma GIF where they originate from, where they're overlayed on top of Friend shaking and changing size. [br] [br]* "Is their name Rorys or Rory?" The answer is both. [br] [br]* The explosion sound and animation they use are the same as Deltarune's. [br] [br]* They wants to kill Friend. [br] [br]* They are the main antagonist of the Super Golden Sigma games.
In WC:TI, Rorys can be difficult to deal with because you often dont have an easy way of avoiding them. In WEGAKILL, this is not the case. You can easily go around them, either by jumping to a different path or by straight up jumping over them. [br] [br]As a result, I wanted Rorys to be more interesting. When implementing them, I had (once again) taken inspiration from ULTRAKILL. ULTRAKILL has a weapon where, if you slam into an enemy at high speed, it'll deal massive damage and send you flying backwards. I LOVE this weapon. So, I decided to do something similar for Rorys, and this was the end result. [br] [br]Small bonus: since the walking code has no concept of acceleration and deceleration while moving, I had to resort to preventing you from moving for a short period when the Rorys Explosion starts.
